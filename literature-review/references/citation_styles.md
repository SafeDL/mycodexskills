# Citation Styles Reference

This document provides detailed guidelines for formatting citations in academic styles commonly used in literature reviews, including GB/T 7714 and IEEE formats for Chinese and English autonomous-driving research manuscripts.

## APA Style (7th Edition)

### Journal Articles

**Format**: Author, A. A., Author, B. B., & Author, C. C. (Year). Title of article. *Title of Periodical*, *volume*(issue), page range. https://doi.org/xx.xxx/yyyy

**Example**: Smith, J. D., Johnson, M. L., & Williams, K. R. (2023). Machine learning approaches in drug discovery. *Nature Reviews Drug Discovery*, *22*(4), 301-318. https://doi.org/10.1038/nrd.2023.001

### Books

**Format**: Author, A. A. (Year). *Title of work: Capital letter also for subtitle*. Publisher Name. https://doi.org/xxxx

**Example**: Kumar, V., Abbas, A. K., & Aster, J. C. (2021). *Robbins and Cotran pathologic basis of disease* (10th ed.). Elsevier.

### Book Chapters

**Format**: Author, A. A., & Author, B. B. (Year). Title of chapter. In E. E. Editor & F. F. Editor (Eds.), *Title of book* (pp. xx-xx). Publisher.

**Example**: Brown, P. O., & Botstein, D. (2020). Exploring the new world of the genome with DNA microarrays. In M. B. Eisen & P. O. Brown (Eds.), *DNA microarrays: A molecular cloning manual* (pp. 1-45). Cold Spring Harbor Laboratory Press.

### Preprints

**Format**: Author, A. A., & Author, B. B. (Year). Title of preprint. *Repository Name*. https://doi.org/xxxx

**Example**: Zhang, Y., Chen, L., & Wang, H. (2024). Novel therapeutic targets in Alzheimer's disease. *bioRxiv*. https://doi.org/10.1101/2024.01.001

### Conference Papers

**Format**: Author, A. A. (Year, Month day-day). Title of paper. In E. E. Editor (Ed.), *Title of conference proceedings* (pp. xx-xx). Publisher. https://doi.org/xxxx

## Nature Style

### Journal Articles

**Format**: Author, A. A., Author, B. B. & Author, C. C. Title of article. *J. Name* **volume**, page range (year).

**Example**: Smith, J. D., Johnson, M. L. & Williams, K. R. Machine learning approaches in drug discovery. *Nat. Rev. Drug Discov.* **22**, 301-318 (2023).

### Books

**Format**: Author, A. A. & Author, B. B. *Book Title* (Publisher, Year).

**Example**: Kumar, V., Abbas, A. K. & Aster, J. C. *Robbins and Cotran Pathologic Basis of Disease* 10th edn (Elsevier, 2021).

### Multiple Authors

- 1-2 authors: List all
- 3+ authors: List first author followed by "et al."

**Example**: Zhang, Y. et al. Novel therapeutic targets in Alzheimer's disease. *bioRxiv* https://doi.org/10.1101/2024.01.001 (2024).

## Chicago Style (Author-Date)

### Journal Articles

**Format**: Author, First Name Middle Initial. Year. "Article Title." *Journal Title* volume, no. issue (Month): page range. https://doi.org/xxxx.

**Example**: Smith, John D., Mary L. Johnson, and Karen R. Williams. 2023. "Machine Learning Approaches in Drug Discovery." *Nature Reviews Drug Discovery* 22, no. 4 (April): 301-318. https://doi.org/10.1038/nrd.2023.001.

### Books

**Format**: Author, First Name Middle Initial. Year. *Book Title: Subtitle*. Edition. Place: Publisher.

**Example**: Kumar, Vinay, Abul K. Abbas, and Jon C. Aster. 2021. *Robbins and Cotran Pathologic Basis of Disease*. 10th ed. Philadelphia: Elsevier.

---

## Vancouver Style (Numbered)

### Journal Articles

**Format**: Author AA, Author BB, Author CC. Title of article. Abbreviated Journal Name. Year;volume(issue):page range.

**Example**: Smith JD, Johnson ML, Williams KR. Machine learning approaches in drug discovery. Nat Rev Drug Discov. 2023;22(4):301-18.

### Books

**Format**: Author AA, Author BB. Title of book. Edition. Place: Publisher; Year.

**Example**: Kumar V, Abbas AK, Aster JC. Robbins and Cotran pathologic basis of disease. 10th ed. Philadelphia: Elsevier; 2021.

### Citation in Text

Use superscript numbers in order of appearance: "Recent studies^1,2^ have shown..."

## GB/T 7714 Style (Chinese National Standard)

Use GB/T 7714 for Chinese journals, Chinese theses, and manuscripts submitted to venues that request the Chinese national bibliographic style. Prefer the version named by the target journal. If no version is specified, use GB/T 7714-2025 for new manuscripts after its implementation date; keep GB/T 7714-2015 when a journal template, LaTeX class, or author guideline still requires it.

### In-Text Citations

**Sequential numeric system (default for most Chinese engineering journals):**
- Single source: 自动驾驶轨迹预测方法已得到广泛研究[1]。
- Multiple sources: 风险场景生成通常结合数据驱动模型与仿真验证[2-4]。
- Author named in text: 王等[5]提出了基于交互风险的轨迹生成方法。

**Author-year system (only when requested):**
- 中文: 王等(2024)提出了安全关键场景生成方法。
- English: Smith et al. (2024) proposed a safety-critical scenario generation method.

### Journal Articles

**Chinese format**: [#] 作者. 题名[J]. 期刊名, 年, 卷(期): 起止页码. DOI.

**Example**: [1] 王某某, 李某某, 张某某. 自动驾驶安全关键场景生成研究综述[J]. 交通运输工程学报, 2024, 24(3): 1-18. DOI:10.xxxx/xxxxx.

**English format**: [#] Author A, Author B, Author C. Title[J]. Journal Name, Year, Volume(Issue): pages. DOI.

**Example**: [2] Smith J, Wang L, Chen Y. Risk-aware trajectory generation for autonomous driving[J]. IEEE Transactions on Intelligent Transportation Systems, 2024, 25(6): 5120-5134. DOI:10.xxxx/xxxxx.

### Conference Papers

**Format**: [#] 作者. 题名[C]//会议论文集名. 出版地: 出版者, 年: 起止页码. DOI.

**Example**: [3] Li X, Zhang H, Liu Y. Adversarial scenario generation for autonomous vehicles[C]//Proceedings of the IEEE Intelligent Vehicles Symposium. Piscataway: IEEE, 2023: 1101-1108. DOI:10.xxxx/xxxxx.

### Books

**Format**: [#] 作者. 书名[M]. 版本项. 出版地: 出版者, 出版年.

**Example**: [4] 王飞跃. 平行驾驶与智能交通[M]. 北京: 科学出版社, 2022.

### Dissertations

**Format**: [#] 作者. 题名[D]. 保存地: 保存单位, 年.

**Example**: [5] 张某某. 自动驾驶车辆交互式轨迹规划方法研究[D]. 北京: 清华大学, 2023.

### Online Resources, Datasets, and Software

Use online-resource fields for datasets, benchmarks, code repositories, and standards when no archival paper is available.

**Format**: [#] 作者或机构. 题名[EB/OL]. (发布日期或更新日期)[引用日期]. URL.

**Example**: [6] Waymo LLC. Waymo Open Dataset[EB/OL]. (2024-01-15)[2026-06-08]. https://waymo.com/open.

### Document Type Codes

Common GB/T codes: journal article [J], monograph/book [M], conference paper [C], dissertation [D], report [R], standard [S], patent [P], database [DB], computer program [CP], electronic bulletin [EB], online [OL].

### Autonomous-Driving Notes for GB/T

- Keep Chinese author names in the order used by the source; do not force initials unless the journal template requires romanized names.
- Preserve original English capitalization for datasets and systems: Waymo Open Dataset, nuScenes, Argoverse, CARLA, SUMO, OpenSCENARIO.
- Include DOI whenever available. For arXiv papers, include arXiv ID or URL if no DOI exists.
- For standards and regulations, use [S] and include the issuing organization, standard number, title, publisher/place when available, and year.

## IEEE Style

IEEE references are numbered in the order they first appear in the manuscript. Use bracketed numeric citations such as [1], [2], [3]-[5]. Do not use author-year citations in IEEE submissions unless a target venue explicitly overrides the IEEE style.

### Journal Articles

**Format**: [#] A. A. Author, B. B. Author, and C. C. Author, "Title of article," *Abbreviated Journal Name*, vol. x, no. x, pp. xxx-xxx, Month Year, doi: xx.xxxx/xxxxx.

**Example**: [1] J. D. Smith, M. L. Johnson, and K. R. Williams, "Machine learning approaches in drug discovery," *Nat. Rev. Drug Discov.*, vol. 22, no. 4, pp. 301-318, Apr. 2023, doi: 10.xxxx/xxxxx.

### Books

**Format**: [#] A. A. Author, *Title of Book*, xth ed. City, State: Publisher, Year.

**Example**: [2] V. Kumar, A. K. Abbas, and J. C. Aster, *Robbins and Cotran Pathologic Basis of Disease*, 10th ed. Philadelphia, PA: Elsevier, 2021.

### Conference Papers

**Format**: [#] A. A. Author and B. B. Author, "Title of paper," in *Proc. Conference Name*, City, Country, Year, pp. xxx-xxx, doi: xx.xxxx/xxxxx.

**Example**: [3] X. Li, H. Zhang, and Y. Liu, "Adversarial scenario generation for autonomous vehicles," in *Proc. IEEE Intell. Veh. Symp. (IV)*, Anchorage, AK, USA, 2023, pp. 1101-1108, doi: 10.xxxx/xxxxx.

### Online Datasets and Software

**Dataset format**: [#] Organization or Author, "Dataset title," version, Year. [Online]. Available: URL. Accessed: Month Day, Year.

**Software format**: [#] Organization or Author, "Software or repository title," version or commit if relevant, Year. [Online]. Available: URL. Accessed: Month Day, Year.

### IEEE Autonomous-Driving Venue Notes

- Use standard IEEE abbreviations where possible: *IEEE Trans. Intell. Transp. Syst.*, *IEEE Trans. Intell. Veh.*, *IEEE Trans. Veh. Technol.*, *IEEE Robot. Autom. Lett.*, *IEEE Trans. Robot.*, *IEEE Access*.
- For ITSC, IV, ICRA, IROS, and CVPR/ICCV/ECCV papers, cite the official proceedings title used by IEEE, ACM, or the conference publisher.
- Include DOI for IEEE Xplore papers. If DOI is absent, include the stable proceedings URL or arXiv identifier.
- Do not abbreviate dataset names unless the paper itself introduces the abbreviation.

## DOI Best Practices

1. **Always verify DOIs**: Use the verify_citations.py script to check all DOIs
2. **Format as URLs**: https://doi.org/10.xxxx/yyyy (preferred over doi:10.xxxx/yyyy)
3. **No period after DOI**: DOI should be the last element without trailing punctuation
4. **Resolve redirects**: Check that DOIs resolve to the correct article

## In-Text Citation Guidelines

### APA Style
- (Smith et al., 2023)
- Smith et al. (2023) demonstrated...
- Multiple citations: (Brown, 2022; Smith et al., 2023; Zhang, 2024)

### Nature Style
- Superscript numbers: Recent studies^1,2^ have shown...
- Or: Recent studies (refs 1,2) have shown...

### Chicago Style
- (Smith, Johnson, and Williams 2023)
- Smith, Johnson, and Williams (2023) found...

### GB/T 7714 Numeric Style
- Use bracketed numbers: 自动驾驶风险评估已有较多研究[1-3]。
- Order references by first appearance in the manuscript.
- Keep Chinese punctuation and spacing consistent with the target journal template.

### IEEE Style
- Use bracketed numbers: autonomous-driving risk assessment has been widely studied [1]-[3].
- Place citations before punctuation when the target IEEE template requires it; otherwise follow the official author guidelines for the chosen venue.

## Reference List Organization

### By Citation Style
- **APA, Chicago**: Alphabetical by first author's last name
- **Nature, Vancouver, IEEE, GB/T 7714 numeric**: Numerical order of first appearance in text
- **GB/T 7714 author-year**: Alphabetical by author or follow the target journal template

### Hanging Indents
Most styles use hanging indents where the first line is flush left and subsequent lines are indented.

### Consistency
Maintain consistent formatting throughout:
- Capitalization (title case vs. sentence case)
- Journal name abbreviations
- DOI presentation
- Author name format
